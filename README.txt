1616 real-data demo

Open index.html to explore 20 randomly selected public LWServers player records from servers 1605, 1616, 1627 and 1636. These are source samples, not applicants. Names, server, alliance, kills and available total hero power come from LWServers snapshots. Missing THP remains unknown. Snapshot retrieval does not guarantee current game values.

Seat colours, capacities, Pending statuses and arrival dates are simulated. Edits stay in the demo; this file is not connected to Google Sheets and has no live sign-in or permission enforcement.
