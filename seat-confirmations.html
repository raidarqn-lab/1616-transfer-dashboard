What to tell people if they ask about a timeline

We expect some confirmations to come through within the next 24 hours, with priority going to key groups and higher-power seats.

Beyond that, it’s difficult to give a firm timeline. Individual white-seat applicants are selected based on strength rather than application order, so a stronger applicant who applies later may be chosen over someone who applied earlier.

Official applications to join the server open in five days. For those on the lower end of power, I recommend having a backup plan, even if we’re your first choice.
