Owner guide: assign usernames and passwords
  1. Open Alliance Hub → Administration → Logins & passwords.
  2. Search for a reviewer’s in-game name and choose the Leadership account. Confirm the linked player is the right person.
  3. Assign a unique username using 3–32 lowercase letters, numbers or underscores. Enter and confirm a password of 12–128 characters and a setup reason.
  4. Choose Save login & password. Give that person their username, password and the Alliance Hub sign-in link privately. Existing passwords cannot be displayed. Reset one if necessary.
  5. Repeat for all 12 reviewers. In Season 4, use View 12 reviewers to check the assigned names. Google-linked leaders can continue using their existing sign-in.
  6. Write in the Notes from RaidARQN column beside any member and click Save note. All 12 reviewers can read these notes, but only you can edit them. These notes are separate from your vote comments and can be updated after voting closes.
  7. Use Close review when voting ends. Only the owner can reopen voting or record final decisions. Disabling password login ends that account’s password sessions; leadership access is managed separately.

Keep votes, comments and exported sheets within leadership. Do not share credentials. Each account represents one reviewer, including ClosetGamer808/HisNationalTreasurer as one person.
